Rules

Do
Pair an icon with its theme name. Icons support the label; they never replace it.
Do
Use one color per icon: ocean on light backgrounds, sky or gold on ocean.
Don't
Use theme icons for research areas, departments or content types. They mean the five themes only.
Don't
Fill, outline, rotate or add a second color to an icon, or scale the stroke with the icon.

Science and engineering for the ocean, the poles and beyond

The Applied Physics Laboratory is a research center of the University of Washington. Our scientists and engineers study how the ocean, ice and atmosphere work, and build the instruments and vehicles to measure them.

What we study

Explore our research
Sound in the sea How sound travels underwater, and how to use it to communicate, navigate and map the seafloor. The moving ocean Waves, currents and mixing, and how the ocean and atmosphere trade heat and energy. Polar regions and a changing climate Sea ice, glaciers and polar oceans, from ice camps and buoys to satellite records. Vehicles, instruments and naval systems Designing, building and testing the tools of ocean science. Ultrasound, photonics and information Physics beyond the ocean, from medical ultrasound to systems for complex data.

Work with us

Federal agencies, industry and fellow researchers partner with APL-UW to answer hard questions at sea, under the ice and in the lab, and to turn the answers into working technology.

Ways to work with us

Most partnerships start with a conversation with a researcher. From there, they usually take one of four forms.

Sponsor research Fund a project with our scientists and engineers, from basic research to applied engineering. Use our facilities Book time on our research vessels, test facilities and shops. License a technology Bring an APL-UW invention to market through UW CoMotion. Use our data Access long-running ocean and polar datasets.

Why partner with APL-UW

We combine a university's science with the engineering staff, shops and ships to take an idea from the whiteboard to the water.
